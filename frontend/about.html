OUR COMPANY

Who we are

Issabub Nigeria Limited is an Ogun-based general construction and civil engineering firm providing comprehensive project execution for residential, commercial, industrial, and infrastructure clients across Nigeria.

Founded on January 28, 2014, the company operates from the Ijoko Ota corridor and manages end-to-end building and civil engineering works from initial site clearance through final handover.

Our dedicated team coordinates specialized subcontractors, site supervisors and engineers across residential developments, civil earthworks and industrial integrations.
